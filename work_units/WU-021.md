# WU-021: Human approval

**Status**: ✅ Complete (2026-10-17) · **Features**: F-001, F-004 · **Estimate**: 4 hours

## Goal

A person can approve a change that stopped at `needs_human` or `escalated`, with a reason, so it merges like any approved change. Opened by WU-020's first editor-made change: every `.plumbix/` edit stops at `needs_human` (rule 5), and a person had only Validate again and Close.

## Context

- A21: `needs_human`, `escalated` and `rejected` are sticky; only a person moves them. `rejected` stays out of scope: it is the validators' explicit no, and a person who disagrees pushes a fix or closes.
- Merge (A15) lands the validated head of the latest finished run. A person approves that head; a later push starts a new iteration and the approval no longer applies.
- Decision recorded in Kai on 2026-10-17: Approve with a reason; merge stays a separate action; moving the pipeline out of `.plumbix/` rejected.

## Design

**Record.** `Change.human_approval` `{reason, head, at}`, set by the approval and cleared when a new run starts (the change enters `running`). Migration 0009 adds the columns. The approval emits `change.state_changed` (to `approved`) and a new event `change.approved_by_person` `{reason, head}`, both in the same transaction.

**Rules.** `Engine.ApproveByPerson(id, reason)`: the reason is required, trimmed, at most 1000 characters; the change must be `needs_human` or `escalated`; the latest run must have finished with a head. Merge accepts a change whose latest finished run is either an approval or the head a person approved.

**API and CLI.** `POST /api/v1/changes/{id}/approve` `{reason}` → 200 `Change` (400 problems, 404, 409 `invalid_state` or `conflict`). `plumbix change approve <id> --reason "<why>"`.

**Web.** On a `needs_human` or `escalated` change the actions show Approve; it opens an inline reason field with Confirm and Keep. The verdict panel shows "Approved by a person · reason · when" while the approval stands, and the merge button appears as for any approval.
