# WU-038: Stage cards fit the window width

**Status**: ✅ Complete (2026-10-19) · **Features**: F-004 · **Estimate**: half a session · **Kai**: 6KZVDT

## Goal

The pipeline editor placed cards on a fixed grid: 360 px each, at least three columns, a chain of dependent steps on one row. A stage was at least about 1,160 px wide, plus 360 px kept free for the inspector, so on most windows the owner scrolled sideways to see a stage. Every step of a stage should be visible across without horizontal scrolling, arranged to the window width; vertical scrolling stays.

## Design

**Layout to a width.** `bands(p, sel, width?)` takes the width available to a stage's grid. When the natural layout fits, nothing changes. When it does not, the column count is what fits at a minimum card width (`MIN_CW`, 240 px), cards share the width up to their full 360 px, and placement wraps: a step whose column would pass the last one starts a new row at the first column, and its chain continues from there. Loose steps fill the rows at that column count. A line to a wrapped step runs from the bottom of the step it needs to the top of the wrapped one, and the "after x" chips still name dependencies across stages. Chip space follows the card width. With no width (tests, first paint), the natural layout is used.

**Measuring.** The page observes the canvas with a `ResizeObserver` and derives the grid width: the canvas minus its padding, the band's padding and border, and the inspector's width while it is open, so opening it reflows the cards instead of pushing them off to the side. The CSS that forced `max-content` widths and the inspector margin goes.
