# WU-065: A repository can be paused or removed

**Status**: ⚪ Planned (2026-10-29) · **Features**: F-001 · **Kai**: QW668B · **Source**: Chart-AI's first-day follow-up, ask 4

A stale registration keeps polling and raises `bypass_commit` for commits that passed the gate on another registration of the same repository. Add `paused` to the repository settings (stops polling and pull-request intake; shown on the card and settable from the CLI) and `DELETE /repos/{id}` (`plumbix repo remove`), refused while a change is open or running, recorded as `repo.removed` naming who asked.
