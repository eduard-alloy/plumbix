# WU-030: Advisory validators

**Status**: ✅ Complete (2026-10-18) · **Features**: F-003, F-004 · **Estimate**: 1 session · **Kai**: SFRKCY · **Decision**: A31

## Goal

A second model can judge every change beside the first without being able to block or approve one. The owner has a Grok profile and wants to compare it with the Claude review on the same changes; today every validator's verdict counts, so a Grok rejection would stop a merge. After this unit a validator step marked `advisory: true` has its verdict recorded in full, tagged, and counted for nothing.

## Design

**Pipeline.** `advisory: true` on a validator step, container or external. Plain steps refuse it. The editor round-trips it.

**Combination.** `validation.ValidatorResult.Advisory` flows from the step. `Combine` carries an advisory result in full: its `ValidatorOutcome`, criteria assessments, findings and risks, each tagged `advisory`. None of it moves the result: no rejection or fix request, no criterion status (`mergeStatus` skips advisory assessments), no human-required risk, no "produced no valid verdict", and an advisory validator alone still means "no validator ran". The target branch's required validators exclude advisory ones, so a branch may drop one.

**Run and API.** `run.Step.Advisory`; `advisory` on `ValidatorOutcome`, `Assessment`, findings and risks in the result; the pipeline editor's step view. OpenAPI documents all of it.

**Web.** Step rows and the verdict's validator list tag advisory steps; a criterion's headline assessment is the first counting one, advisory assessments sit under "more" with the tag; findings and risks show the tag next to the validator's name; the pipeline editor has an Advisory switch on validators.
